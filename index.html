📊 Flatness Reading

How uniform and hedged the prose reads. Lower is more varied and more human-sounding.

This is a style reading, not an AI detector. It measures sentence-rhythm uniformity, punctuation density, stock constructions and abstract-noun load — the things a human reader notices. Thresholds are calibrated against 1,283 human-written passages (19th-century literary prose and contemporary expository writing): half of them score 9 or below, and only 1% reach “leaning flat”. It does not predict what any detection service will say about your text, and a low score guarantees nothing. Use it the way you'd use a readability score: as a prompt to reread, not as a verdict.
